About This Platform

This Cyber Threat Intelligence and Risk Analytics Platform collects threat intelligence from URLhaus and Feodo Tracker, validates and normalizes indicators through a Python ETL pipeline, stores structured records in SQLite, and presents analytical insights through an interactive Flask dashboard. The platform supports threat categorization, investigation-priority risk scoring, data analysis, and monitoring of collected threat intelligence.
